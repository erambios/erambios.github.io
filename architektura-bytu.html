Architecture of Being: From Illusion to Truth

Core Authorial Thesis

Human perception is surrounded by layers of structural deception—cultural narratives, biological biases, and linguistic limitations that obscure objective reality. The treatise Architecture of Being outlines the methodical transition from these perceptual illusions toward fundamental truth.

To reach ontological clarity, consciousness must dismantle comfortable abstractions and examine the underlying machinery of existence. This process requires deconstructing false paradigms before a resilient framework of thought can be constructed.

True understanding is not achieved by accumulating opinions, but by systematically dismantling deception until only unyielding, objective reality remains.
