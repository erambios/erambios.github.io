Manifesto of Ontological Malicology

Core Authorial Thesis

Ontological Malicology posits that reality possesses an inherent cognitive friction—a structural "malice" that reveals itself whenever human consciousness attempts to approach fundamental truth.

This resistance is not personal or moral; rather, it manifests as a cognitive veil where the closer one moves toward understanding the essence of being, the more elusive and contradictory the universe appears.

Knowledge is gained not through passive observation, but through overcoming this active epistemological friction. True inquiry requires recognizing this structural obstacle and deliberately cutting through the illusions created by perceptual noise.
