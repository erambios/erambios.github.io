Manifesto of Ontological Malicology

Epistemological Friction & Cognitive Resistance

Ontological Malicology posits that reality exhibits a structural, cognitive friction whenever an inquiring mind approaches absolute, unvarnished truth.

This resistance is not personal; it is an inherent property of perceptual architecture. To uncover truth, one must anticipate this systemic friction and overcome the cognitive illusions designed to preserve comfort over clarity.
