Thesis of Solitary Amoralism

Core Authorial Thesis

Moral systems are overwhelmingly constructed as collective contracts designed to regulate social structures. The Thesis of Solitary Amoralism examines the stance of the isolated consciousness operating outside collective social pressures and cultural expectations.

Detached from social conditioning, conventional moral dictates lose their external authority. The solitary individual must confront the raw reality of choice and action without relying on performative virtue or collective consensus.

Solitary Amoralism is not an invitation to cruelty, but an assertion of radical self-governance—a state wherein responsibility is grounded purely in conscious intent rather than external societal judgment.
