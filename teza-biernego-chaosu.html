Thesis of Passive Chaos

Core Authorial Thesis

Chaos is not an active, malicious, or destructive force operating within the universe; rather, it represents the default, neutral state of natural energy dissipation. It is the baseline reality of unguided existence—a constant movement toward entropy when external structure is absent.

Because chaos is inherently passive, order cannot occur spontaneously or sustain itself without effort. Every structure, every system of logic, and every moral architecture is an active act of intellectual and existential resistance against this natural dissipation.

To establish order is to wage a deliberate battle against the passive gravity of reality. Reason and consciousness serve as the primary mechanisms through which order asserts itself against the indifferent tide of chaos.
